# Proposed CPU recognition reset

17 October 2026. The human rejects the previous development plan and confirms that no stronger computer is available. This is a proposal for the next implementation, not evidence that a new recognizer works. The existing live service remains a flow-testing baseline. No new dependency, dataset acquisition or model training is started by this document.

## Decision

Keep the working upload/API/original-frame evidence infrastructure. Stop broad model/feature searches, attempts to validate a model with its own guesses, and the assumption that a generic handover class is the banking target. Build one demonstrably visible, source-grounded cash-workflow capability before expanding the catalogue. The supplied positive label and anchor help locate a candidate event but do not identify its type or prove that an entire four-stage transaction is visible.

The laptop can run the installed temporal model: the real-MOV probe completed eight recordings and 149 windows with a longest-source repeat in 74.82229420099611 seconds. Its action interpretation remains unvalidated. Hardware is sufficient for a bounded experiment with a small supervised head; it does not establish that this encoder or a larger training corpus will solve recognition.

## First milestone: one checked end-to-end capability

1. **Establish what differs in the actual case examples.** A person watching locally checks a few development examples and counterexamples at their original detail. Record observable action intervals, transaction context when known and uncertainty. Use document presentation, cash movement/counting, document processing and completion as possible context; do not turn a missing stage in a partial recording into a violation. Select one action/rule family only after a visible distinction is confirmed. A short local review session is the requested human contribution; no bank pixels, clip descriptions or labels enter chat.
2. **Test the loss-of-detail hypothesis.** Retain the scene view and add up to two deterministic, motion-selected detail views with source coordinates/times preserved. Crop selection must not use targets, organizer anchors or a fixed cashier location that fails a changed camera angle. Freeze this as a separate version; do not alter or overwrite the approved direct probe or the existing label-gated transfer experiment. The current 172-pixel full-frame letterbox leaves only roughly 94 pixels across the original scene. Crops may help, but they must earn their place through the checked examples.
3. **Fit a small local recognizer only if the representation distinguishes the action.** Reuse installed components initially. Learn a narrow action from independently reviewed examples and hard negatives, with preprocessing fitted inside training folds. Train only the small classifier initially. Do not require backbone fine-tuning or a large external corpus. If the selected representation loses the action detail, stop that recipe and reassess the visual component instead of fitting more classifiers to it.
4. **Apply contextual event logic.** Combine supported observations over time and generate the original-frame evidence. Associate a public rule only when its observable conditions and transaction context are established. Counting presence, document presence and cash transfer are observations; they do not by themselves establish identity verification, a correct amount, lawful ownership, authenticity or a violation. Respect packaged-withdrawal and electronic-document exceptions.
5. **Decide from a frozen independent check.** Measure action misses/false alarms, time alignment, evidence relevance, repeatability and actual end-to-end runtime on distinct reviewed development sources. Preserve the existing protocol, protected recordings and promotion gates. The existing sixteen-recording transfer pilot's requirements are not silently relaxed; a changed target/representation requires its own predeclared version. No repeated tuning against blind examples or the reserved eight is permitted.

## Work budget and stopping rule

Aim to produce the first checked capability within two hours of obtaining usable action examples, with approximately 15–25 minutes of focused local human input where the recordings allow it. These are planning estimates, not measured performance or a promise that the first family is learnable. Complete the required train/sanity checks after feature/model changes and report real before/after results.

Stop expansion if the action cannot be reliably distinguished from its counterexamples, its rule conditions cannot be established, or the complete accepted-video flow cannot fit the five-minute requirement. Then report the exact failed condition and narrow the claimed capability. A weak perception model cannot be repaired by adding more rules.

External labelled examples are a later, targeted supplement after a specific missing action is identified. Larger CPU vision-language models remain an optional bounded experiment; loading into RAM would not prove exhaustive video throughput or recognition quality. No stronger computer, external bank-data upload, cloud inference or cloud training is assumed.

## Success and limits

The deliverable is one repeatable event recognizer with independently checked examples, original evidence and defensible rule applicability. It is the basis for expanding to other families, not complete case coverage or a forecast of 95–100 jury points. The current data and measurements do not support that forecast.
