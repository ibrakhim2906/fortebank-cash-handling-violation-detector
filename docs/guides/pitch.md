# Guide: pitch (humans own this; Codex drafts material)

From the organizers' own workshop: the best model doesn't win. Every requirement covered, depth over extra features, a simple interface, slides that show the work.

## 6-slide structure
1. **The decision** — ForteBank's problem in one line. Don't explain the problem at length; the jury knows it.
2. **The insight** — one finding others missed, with one number and one chart. This is the centre of the pitch.
3. **The model and why to trust it** — validation that mimics real use, leakage checks passed, baseline → final score.
4. **Why it decides** — reasons for one real client in plain words.
5. **Impact in ₸** — headline number + assumptions + adjustable threshold.
6. **Demo + how ForteBank deploys it** — who uses it, where it plugs in, next step.

Checklist: every box in `CHECKLIST.md` visibly ticked somewhere in slides or demo.
No tech-stack slide. Name a niche technique only if it fixed something, and show what it fixed.

## Timing
- Prepare a 2-minute and a 5-minute version. The real slot may be ~1.5–2 minutes.
- 2-minute script ≈ 260–300 spoken words.
- Rehearse at least 5 times with a timer. The best speaker presents.
- Plan one memorable moment (live demo on one client, or the surprising insight).

## Q&A — prepare short honest answers to
1. How do you know there's no leakage?
2. How would it work on new clients / next quarter?
3. Why this threshold? What if costs change?
4. Is it explainable to a regulator? Is it fair across groups?
5. What happens when data drifts? How would you monitor it?
6. What are the limitations of the data?
7. Why not a simpler / more complex model?
8. How long to deploy? What does ForteBank need to provide?
If asked about weak spots, answer honestly. Don't bring them up yourself.
