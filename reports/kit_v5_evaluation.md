## Corrections required before activation

1. **Use actual compute and budget.** The proposal assumes approximately 40 GB laptop RAM and a paid 24 GB GPU pod; inspected WSL has 18 GiB and no GPU, and the human confirms API credits only. A $40–60 rental and self-hosted vLLM are neither available nor authorized. The service can target a GPU later, but its current execution path must be measured on available hardware. See candidate `architecture.md:18`, `deploy.md:28` and `GAMEPLAN.md:10`.

2. **Do not mistake proxies for facts.** An illuminated display does not prove unlocked access; a person near equipment is not necessarily unauthorized; close wrists do not prove a cash transfer; no counter motion does not mean no manual counting. The enabled screen rule would catch correct screen-change examples Х-23/Х-29; the four-second no-client/no-counter cash rule can catch ordinary solo counting. See candidate `rules.seed.json:32`, `rules.seed.json:187`, `rules_engine.md:28–36` and `violations.md:42`. Require explicit true/false/unknown observations, calibrated visibility and separate transaction/role context.

3. **Guard omission claims and degraded operation.** Three seconds of a censored/occluded interval cannot establish a missing ID/receipt/counting step. A failed or skipped auditor must not silently become `no_violation` for checks only it can perform. Repeated calls to the same model are not independent confirmation. Keep capability coverage internally and fail when a required analysis cannot be completed. See candidate `rules_engine.md:65`, `auditor.md:54–66`.

4. **Reconcile legal text and exceptions.** Keep the verified temporary №14§16 edition through 01.01.2027. Update №231 to its current text, including digital-ID and packaged-cash exceptions. Door/transfer-device installation requirements do not automatically substantiate every proposed behavioral ban. Do not fabricate an unspecified ForteBank internal regulation for reading or unlocked-screen events. Food/drink interpretation and unresolved key/security requirements must stay qualified. The current source verification is in `docs/action_rule_candidates.md`; do not replace it with stale or shortened quotations. Nine enabled seed rules are not nine validated capabilities.

5. **Repair evaluation.** Preserve full-content SHA-256 duplicate groups; a 16×28 single-frame fingerprint is unsafe for merging similar staged clips. Use one-to-one event matching and report event false positives, failures and coverage, not only the nearest prediction to each label. Fit time offsets only on development calibration data and evaluate separately. All 40 recordings were already reviewed; a new split or held-setup report cannot erase that selection history. Augmentations are useful stress tests, not evidence of a new camera angle. Training recall 1.0 is a development target, not a generalization claim.

6. **Remove unsupported checker assumptions.** Bilingual names, universal onset timestamps, ±1–2-second tolerances and a three-event cap are team choices, not verified scoring rules. Bilingual text is not guaranteed to pass exact string matching. Return all distinct supported events; the official 1–3 limit concerns evidence frames per event. The kit also conflicts between chronological and confidence ordering. Use one documented policy and disclose uncertainty. A claim of approximately five detection points per video assumes exactly five equally weighted tests, while the brief says 5+ and provides no such formula.

7. **Avoid a rewrite and stack expansion.** The candidate says to ignore `src/`, but this repository now contains the actual tested video pipeline there. Reuse authentication, uploads, decoding, evidence, protocol provenance and existing offline assets. Preserve `src/eval.py`, raw data, API keys, labels, decisions and uncommitted work. Keep source paths through configuration; do not unzip over the raw delivery. Add pose, OWLv2/OpenCLIP, ONNX Runtime or vLLM only for a demonstrated gap with exact dependency approval. For the pinned OpenCV Zoo YOLOX asset, preserve its official RGB preprocessing; the kit's BGR prescription is wrong for that asset.
