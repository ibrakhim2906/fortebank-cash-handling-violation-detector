## Source grounding

- [Current NBK №231](/home/ibrak/datathon-kit/docs/action_rule_candidates.md): §12 personal-item storage; §§19–20 acceptance/counting/current-client cash and prior-cash storage; §22 departure during unfinished acceptance service; §§24–25 collection-bag checks and conditional opening/recount; §80 controlled key/stamp arrangements. Transaction context and exceptions must match.
- [Current NBK №14](https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/by-ngr/V2000020075?language=rus): cash-room premises and lockable doors, including §§16/19. A lockable-door requirement is not itself a universal behavioral must-stay-closed clause. Old №250/2012 is repealed.
- [Halyk public employee guidance](https://halykbank.kz/en/for-employees): clean-desk document storage on departure. Attribute this as Halyk practice, not ForteBank's policy or an RK-wide obligation.
- [ARRFR №53/2026](https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/by-ngr/V2600038336?language=rus): information-security context. Its inactivity timeout does not prove an immediate short-departure lock violation. No current categorical cabinet-key-left-in-lock ban or standalone food ban was verified.

## Implication for the backend

Use a context-aware visual observer and explicit temporal conditions for these concrete procedural scenarios. Preserve sufficiently sharp desk/hand, storage, doorway and screen views plus whole-scene context; infer the workstation role from the scene, not a person's identity, clothing color or a fixed side of the image. Track entry/closure, service start/end, counting/storage and departure/object persistence. Keep “other/unclear” observations for unseen scenarios.

First qualify visible-state observations against contrasting examples: door closure, cash left on desk after exit, storage left open, personal-item placement and work-screen change. Add service/counting omission claims only with adequate temporal coverage and applicable exceptions. Do not retrain a generic activity classifier or turn this catalogue into self-verified gold. A later new inference recipe must be frozen and measured against the official binary labels, with classification/evidence/source evaluation kept separate. Newly reviewed former holdout examples cannot validate generalization independently.
