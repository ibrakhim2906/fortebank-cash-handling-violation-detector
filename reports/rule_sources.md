# Public normative research leads — P1

Research date: 2026-10-17. No footage, frames or footage descriptions were used in this research. These are candidate source documents, not established mappings to observed events or legal conclusions.

## Cash operations

[NBK Board resolution of 29 November 2019 №231, registration №19680](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680) approves rules for cash and collection operations in banks and covered organizations.

Official indexed [Adilet comparison text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1900019680/compare) provides candidate sections: paragraph 15 (recording cash transfers between cash workers), paragraph 19(5–6) (deposit documents/confirmation), paragraph 31(5–6) (withdrawal documents/confirmation).

Limits: a missing visible piece of paper in a short clip cannot establish a violation. Digital records, offscreen actions and the transaction stage can matter. These sections require a complete current-text/context check before implementing a narrowly observable rule.

## Cash premises and physical controls

[NBK Board resolution of 24 February 2020 №14, registration №20075](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075) concerns guarding and arrangement of covered bank/exchange/cash premises.

Official indexed [Adilet comparison text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075/compare) provides candidate paragraph 8 (cash-unit isolation and service entrance) and paragraph 16(1,3–4) (cashier entrance, protective opening and transfer device).

Limits: these are installation/layout requirements. A momentarily open door does not itself establish an unauthorized action. Video generally cannot establish certification, lock functionality, structural dimensions or a person's authorization. Do not turn an installation rule into a fabricated behavioral prohibition.

## Effective-date caution

[NBK Board resolution of 24 December 2024 №88, registration №35552](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035552) amends №14. The [official indexed text](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035552) contains paragraph 5(4), which suspends replacement paragraph 16 wording until 1 January 2027 and supplies interim wording, including lockable entrances and transfer devices with controls inside the cashier area.

The [NBK information notice of 20 January 2025](https://nationalbank.kz/ru/news/informacionnye-soobshcheniya/17309) corroborates amendments and the transition. The actual act and effective-date provisions govern; the notice is contextual material.

For 17 October 2026, do not assume future glass/certification wording is already operative. Check subsequent amendments and the complete effective version before activating any rule.
