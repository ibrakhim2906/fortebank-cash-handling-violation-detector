# External video datasets — verified shortlist, 17 October 2026

**Best large-corpus lead: HoloAssist. Best immediate route: reuse a pretrained local observer and adapt narrowly to the supplied human-reviewed bank targets, rather than train a video foundation model from scratch.** The search found useful human manipulation/procedure datasets with commercial-use-compatible data terms, but did not verify a large, openly licensed cash-desk video corpus with the case's violation labels. That is a bounded search result, not a claim that none exists.

Scope: public project, author repository, dataset and license pages only. No corpora, model weights or packages were downloaded; no bank data was sent to these sites. Publisher-listed access paths were inspected, not successfully downloaded/tested. Licensing statements below concern dataset content, not merely code. Model weights and third-party body models require separate checks.

## Very large, but conditional or poorly matched

**Ego4D:** official documentation reports3,600h of narrated first-person daily/work activity and hand-object benchmarks. Its official license preview **does allow commercial product development and model use**, subject to the agreements; it is incorrect to dismiss it simply as noncommercial. Raw video redistribution/third-party access is restricted, and signed agreements govern actual use. The project says access approval can take48h; the downloader describes roughly5TB full-scale data with filters. This is a useful longer-term source, but access and scale are poor fits for the remaining<16h. [Official overview](https://ego4d-data.org/docs/), [license preview](https://ego4d-data.org/pdfs/Ego4D-Licenses-Draft.pdf), [access](https://ego4d-data.org/), [download filters/size](https://github.com/facebookresearch/Ego4d/blob/main/ego4d/cli/README.md). No agreement was signed and no access was requested here.

**DROID:**76K robot trajectories/350h across564 scenes and86 tasks are impressive scale, but robot grippers/policies and language instructions are a weak match to human cashier actions. The authors' paper states CC BY4.0 for the full dataset. Do not spend this deadline on general robot-policy training. [Official project](https://droid-dataset.github.io/), [author paper](https://www.jiajunwu.com/papers/droid_rss.pdf).

## Cash-specific lead: important limitation

**Microsoft BankNote-Net** releases approximately24.8K **256-dimensional embeddings**,17 currencies and112 denominations, plus a pretrained encoder. The README contains slightly inconsistent24,816/24,826 totals. It explicitly uses **CDLA-Permissive-2.0 for the dataset**; the MIT code badge is not its data license. This is not a large raw-image or video-action release, and KZT is not in its listed currency choices. It may be a later banknote-representation experiment, but does not teach counting sequence, cash-versus-document localization, receipt issuance or storage obligations. Do not reconstruct protected high-resolution banknotes from embeddings. The encoder's exact artifact/license/runtime must be pinned separately before adoption; its documented Keras setup is not the currently approved LiteRT inference path. [Official dataset/model README](https://github.com/microsoft/banknote-net#banknote-net-open-dataset-for-assistive-currency-recognition), [data/model license section](https://github.com/microsoft/banknote-net#license-for-dataset-and-model).

## Attractive sources excluded from the preferred commercial shortlist

- **Assembly101:**4,321 multiview assembly videos, over1M fine action segments; author page explicitly specifies **CC BY-NC4.0**. [Project and dataset license](https://assembly-101.github.io/#license).
- **EPIC-KITCHENS:** fine hand/object actions, but the official dataset page specifies **CC BY-NC4.0** and directs commercial-license requests to the authors. [Official copyright](https://epic-kitchens.github.io/2026).
- **Something-Something V2:**220,847 clips/174 labels,19.4GB published video archive; Qualcomm explicitly offers it for research under a research-use agreement. Good manipulation semantics do not override those terms. [Official dataset/license](https://www.qualcomm.com/developer/software/something-something-v-2-dataset).
- **RetailAction:**21K multiview samples/approximately41h and take/put/touch labels are spatially relevant. However its custom license requires a further license above$10K product/service revenue, and the publisher's organization page describes research/noncommercial availability. Do not call this unrestricted commercial data or make it the default without resolving the intended bank-commercial use. [Official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/standard-cognition/RetailAction), [dataset-specific license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/standard-cognition/RetailAction/blob/fd1b92a57c772b7fab8d53b5daa56730cd3bd48c/LICENSE), [publisher notice](https://huggingface.co/standard-cognition).

## Practical decision for CPU/18GiB and this deadline

The following is an engineering recommendation, not a benchmark result:

1. **Continue the approved bank-specific checkpoint now.** The imported40-recording/14-type human annotation layer is much closer to the actual target than cooking/robot labels. Use sufficiently sharp scene/detail sequences, qualified rules and independent source-frame evidence. All supplied clips are now development data; external data does not restore an untouched bank holdout.
2. **Reuse learned weights; do not relearn general vision from the whole corpus.** The existing local MoViNet/LiteRT, OpenCV/NumPy and scikit-learn stack can extract features sequentially and fit a small head without new dependencies. Its already observed recognition limitations still apply; extra labels cannot recover details destroyed by preprocessing. Generic procedural-error predictions must not become bank-violation labels.
3. **If a measured failure calls for external supplementation, HoloAssist is first choice for a bounded, action-matched subset; CaptainCook4D is second for sequence/error structure.** First inspect public annotation metadata to identify actual overlapping actions. Select RGB-supported targets, excluding labels whose interpretation requires dialogue or audio, since the case is visual-only. Freeze one small selection and one encoder/head recipe, cap acquisition/extraction time, and compare against the same bank diagnostic. Do not download144GB simply because169h sounds promising. TACO is an alternative only when the missing capability is hand-object relation recognition.
4. **Keep external provenance and target semantics separate.** Record exact source release, video/annotation hashes and applicable data licenses; retain original external labels. Train only genuinely shared observable primitives. Do not mark a normal external action as a bank violation or turn automatic proposed labels into human truth. Any later weight reuse requires its own commercial license and runtime check.

**Additional dependencies required for this research/recommended initial adaptation path: none.** Full published training stacks or new checkpoint formats may require additional packages/GPU capacity, but no such adoption is specified or approved here. No model training, new download or measured score improvement is claimed. A large unrelated corpus is not evidence for a95–100 jury outcome.
